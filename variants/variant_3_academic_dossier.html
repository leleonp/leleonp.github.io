Biography & Scientific Vision

I am a scientist driven by innovative inquiry and methodical rigor. Throughout my career in both academic institutions (UNAB, U. de Chile, Fraunhofer) and industrial biotechnology leadership (Codebreaker Technologies), I have specialized in formulating bold scientific hypotheses, building production-grade bioinformatic infrastructures, and translating complex genomic datasets into validated discoveries.

My technical work focuses on cloud workflow orchestration with Nextflow DSL2, containerized microservices (Docker/Singularity), high-performance computing (AWS Batch / GCP), and statistical modeling with R/Bioconductor and Python.
